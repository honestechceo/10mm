Where's My 10MM?!

Privacy Policy · Effective November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Where's My 10MM?!" (the "App"), made by Honestech Mobile Mechanic ("we"), collects and how it is used.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers that store information about you. Your game progress (levels, coins, score, finishes and settings) is saved on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the iPhone app also backs up that same game progress to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it can be restored on your other devices. We cannot see or access your iCloud data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device such as the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this as described in its policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • On iPhone, the App asks for permission before any tracking (Apple App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will still see ads, but they won't be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EU/UK), the App shows a consent form. You can change your choice any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the full-screen ads.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information. Apple shares with the App only whether a purchase was made, so your Remove Ads purchase can be restored.

Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes the game data stored on your device. A copy may remain in your iCloud account until you remove it in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. You can also reset progress in Settings → Progress. To reset your advertising identifier, use your iPhone's Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
