Common questions

How do I play?
Tap JUMP to jump. Tap again in the air to double-jump, and tap quickly a third time to fly. Reach the goal at the end of each level (roll under, land on top, or jump into the open drawer) before the mechanic's glove or the magnet catches you.
I bought Remove Ads on another phone. How do I get it back?
Open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.
There's no sound.
Check that the Silent switch is off and the volume is up, then make sure Sound effects and Music are ON in Settings.
I got a new iPhone. Where's my progress?
Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on, install the game, and open it. Your coins, finishes and levels come back automatically. Settings shows "Cloud save: iCLOUD ON" when backup is working. Remove Ads comes back with Settings → Restore purchase.
How do I start over?
Settings → Progress → RESET, then tap again to confirm. Skins you've unlocked stay unlocked.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Settings → Ad privacy. See the Privacy Policy for details.
